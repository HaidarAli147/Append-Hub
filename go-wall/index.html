Keep the match moving. Goaaal tracks football, handball, and tennis on one phone, works in Arabic and English, and keeps running offline.

READY FOR THE GAME

Everything the sideline needs.

Football

Match clock, stoppage time, goals, cards, and undo.

Handball

Countdown periods with suspensions that follow play time.

Tennis

Sets, tie-breaks, and automatic serve rotation.

No account or installation is required. Scores and settings are stored on this device. The scoreboard can continue offline after the app has loaded once.
